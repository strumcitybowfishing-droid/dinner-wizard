# DINNER WIZARD — TikTok / YouTube Shorts

**Runtime:** ~28 seconds (hard cap 45)  
**Format:** 9:16 vertical, phone-native  
**VO:** tired-but-funny wife in her kitchen. Talk to the camera like the kids are in the next room. Not a brand announcer.  
**Music:** quiet kitchen (fridge hum, a pan, a tap) + a little sparkle when the hat shows. No stock “upbeat lifestyle.”  
**End card:** URL on screen the whole last beat.  
**Do not say:** Line & Dock. Do not fake celebrity quotes.

Live site: **https://dinner-wizard.onrender.com**

---

## Setup

Kitchen, about 5:40 p.m. Fridge light. One phone. Optional off-camera husband: “I don’t care. Whatever you want.” She is done being the menu.

Film the app on a real phone. Dark green UI, gold skillet-hat icon. Do not mock up fake screens.

    (main-course category listing was rate-limited; stew batch kept)

Not used: MeatEater, AllRecipes, Food Network, NYT, ATK, BBC, copyrighted
cookbooks, TheMealDB (already harvested), USDA MyPlate Kitchen (already in usda.json).

without structured ingredient lists, so they were not imported.
Dropped after fetch: desserts, drinks, breakfast pancakes/muffins/oatmeal,
side salads, dips, sauces, seasoning mixes, and incomplete pages.
